Probabilistic Method

PhD / masters Course, 5hp, Autumn 2026, Uppsala University

This course covers the theoretical basis for random graphs and general techniques to analyse random discrete structures.

Topics to include : Random graph models, First and Second Moment methods, Thresholds / Phase transitions, Analysis of Random Algorithms, Alterations, Lovász Local Lemma, Graphons / Graph limits, Correlation Inequalities, Janson Inequalities, Concentration of Measure, Non-concentration of Measure, Entropy methods.

Lecturers:

Anda Skeja (homepage) and Fiona Skerman (homepage)

Please contact Fiona to be added to the course page on studium. This is a PhD / master's course running Sep - Dec this year.
